# ND Riot — SEO & GEO Strategy Report

**Site:** https://www.ndriot.com
**Prepared:** July 30, 2026
**Scope:** Keyword research, on-page SEO audit, GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) readiness, and a homepage H1 recommendation.

---

## 1. Business Summary

ND Riot is a discovery platform for **real independent comics** and the
creators behind them. It functions as part directory, part advocate, and part
network — spanning graphic novels, single issues, and webcomics across genres
including sci-fi, action/adventure, horror, humor/satire, and all-ages, plus
crowdfunding for indie comics.

Audience is twofold: **readers** looking to discover indie comics, and
**creators** looking to publish and be found.

## 3. On-Page SEO Audit

### Fixed (verified on re-scan)
- **Unique titles per page** — e.g., `The seed witch · ND Riot`,
  `Indie Comic Makers · ND Riot`, `John Haney · ND Riot`.
- **Canonical tags** present sitewide, pointing to correct URLs.
- **Unique meta descriptions** on comic detail pages (pulled from synopsis).
- Clean URL architecture (`/books/…`, `/creators/…`, `/editorial`).
- robots.txt present; sitemap.xml referenced and reachable.

### Still outstanding
- **Genre/format hub pages do not exist** (404 on /genres, /genre/sci-fi, etc.).
- **Missing meta descriptions** on the creators listing and editorial pages.
- **Image alt text incomplete** — 9/33 missing on homepage, 2/10 on comic page.
- Homepage **H1 is a slogan**, not keyword-bearing (see §5).
- No **/about** or **/faq** page yet.

## 4. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Audit

GEO = being citable/quotable by AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity,
Google AI Overviews).

### Fixed (verified on re-scan)
- **Structured data implemented well:**
  - Homepage: `Organization` + `WebSite` (Organization has logo, url, and
    `sameAs` links to Instagram, Threads, Discord).
  - Comic pages: `Book` (author, image, description, genre) + `BreadcrumbList`.
  - Creator pages: `Person` + `BreadcrumbList`.

### Still outstanding
- **No genre hub / ItemList pages** for listicle-style AI queries.
- **No FAQPage schema** (highest-value GEO content type).
- **No Article schema** on editorial posts (your best citable asset).
- **No entity-defining "What is ND Riot?" passage** for AI to quote directly.

## 5. Homepage H1 Recommendation

**Current:** `REAL COMIC INDEPENDENTS` — on-brand but contains no searched phrase.

**Recommended:**
> **Discover Real Independent Comics, Graphic Novels & Webcomics**

Leads with the brand promise, then works in two high-volume, low-competition
head terms. Keep the H1 distinct from the title tag; maintain exactly one H1.

**Alternatives:**
- *Discover Independent Comics & the Creators Behind Them* (creator emphasis)
- *Find & Read Truly Independent Comics, Graphic Novels & Webcomics* (intent-led)
- *Real Independent Comics, Made by Real Indie Creators* (closest to current voice)
